02. Architecture & Reliability Mindset

How I approach designing and maintaining production systems in a team environment.

Scaling & State

I prioritize stateless services, horizontal scaling, and designing appropriate data models over vertical monoliths. Caching is applied judiciously at hot paths to protect databases.

Failure Handling

Systems fail. I build for resilience: enforcing idempotent APIs for safe retries, applying circuit breakers, timeouts, and graceful fallbacks during downstream outages.

Performance Tuning

I rely on metrics, not guesses. Optimization means analyzing slow queries, applying proper database indexing, profiling hot JVM paths, and measuring the real-world impact.

Maintainable Design

I establish clean domain boundaries, enforce modular service design, and utilize message-driven asynchronous workflows to keep systems decoupled and maintainable for the long term.

04. Engineering Case Studies

Multi-Tenant B2B Rewards Platform (CacEasy)

NirmanNext

Business Context

NirmanNext needed to increase product retention and drive repeat B2B orders by directly incentivizing end-contractors via a highly available QR-code scanning rewards system.

My Responsibility

Owned the backend architecture. Designed the core services and databases, and collaborated actively with frontend and product teams to integrate the scanning application into the central platform workflows.

Architecture & Technical Decisions

Architected a modular Java/Spring Boot service handling concurrent scan ingests. Utilized a highly optimized MySQL schema to enforce transaction integrity on reward ledgers, applying caching to prevent thundering herd issues on high-velocity reward queries.

Reliability & Performance Impact

The system supported +80% repeat orders without degradation. Automated ledger reconciliations reduced manual operational interventions by 90%.

What I’d do differently today:

If redesigning this today, I would introduce a message queue (like RabbitMQ or Kafka) between the QR scanning edge service and the rewards calculation engine to decouple peak traffic spikes and smooth out load during high-volume promotional days.

Automated Mortgage Workflow Pipeline

XL Dynamics LMS

Business Context

Loan processors were spending hours manually verifying compliance rules and calculating parameters, slowing down loan origination and increasing the risk of expensive human error.

My Responsibility

Architecture & Technical Decisions

Implemented async background jobs to evaluate loans against dynamic rulesets. Re-designed underlying transactional tables for faster read-heavy querying without locking write paths. Used Spring Batch for high-volume overnight reconciliations.

Reliability & Performance Impact

Automated checks reduced loan turnaround time by 33% and replaced highly manual workflows, saving ~$350K/month in operational costs and heavily reducing compliance errors.

What I’d do differently today:

Today, I would add structured logging and centralized correlated tracing (like OpenTelemetry) from the start to make debugging and incident analysis much faster when a specific loan gets stuck in a complex state transition.
