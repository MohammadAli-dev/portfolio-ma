02. Architecture & Reliability Mindset

How I approach designing and maintaining production systems in a team environment.

Scaling & State

I prioritize stateless services, horizontal scaling, and appropriate data modeling over vertical monoliths. Caching is applied judiciously at hot paths to protect databases.

Failure Handling

Systems fail. I build for resilience: enforcing idempotent APIs for safe retries, applying circuit breakers, timeouts, and graceful fallbacks during downstream outages.

Performance Tuning

I rely on metrics, not guesses. Optimization means analyzing slow queries, applying proper database indexing, profiling hot JVM paths, and measuring real-world impact.

Maintainable Design

I establish clean domain boundaries, enforce modular service design, and utilize message-driven asynchronous workflows to decouple latency-sensitive tasks.

04. Engineering Case Studies

Multi-Tenant B2B Rewards Platform

NirmanNext (CacEasy)

Business Context

NirmanNext needed to increase product retention and drive repeat B2B orders by directly incentivizing end-contractors via a highly available QR-code scanning rewards system.

My Responsibility

Architected the core APIs and databases. Collaborated actively with frontend and product teams to integrate the scanning application into central platform workflows.

Architecture & Decisions

Built a modular Java/Spring Boot service for concurrent scan ingests. Utilized an optimized MySQL schema to enforce transaction integrity on reward ledgers, applying caching to prevent thundering herd issues.

Reliability & Impact

Supported +80% repeat orders without degradation. Automated ledger reconciliations reduced manual operational interventions by 90%.

What I’d do differently today:

I would introduce a message queue (e.g., Kafka/RabbitMQ) between the QR edge service and the rewards calculation engine to decouple peak traffic spikes during high-volume promotional days.
